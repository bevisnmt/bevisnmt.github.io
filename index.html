04 / Reflection

What this work shows

I use Situation, Task, Action, and Result (STAR) to explain the work and the skills I am still developing.

Virtualization lab

From concepts to a working container

SituationVirtual machines and containers were new to me.

TaskFollow the Proxmox lab and understand what changes when deploying a container.

ActionI worked through container settings and compared its shared kernel with a virtual machine’s guest OS.

Result & learningI can explain that difference more clearly now. My next step is to document a complete setup and troubleshoot it without relying on lab instructions.

Connectivity audit

Seeing the networks behind everyday tasks

SituationI used online services throughout the day without tracking how much I depended on them.

TaskLog a day of network use and analyze two service outages.

ActionI recorded nine sessions and compared the Meta and AT&T outages using published accounts of what happened.

Result & learningI saw how one infrastructure failure can disrupt many activities. Reviewing my log also corrected my original time estimate: the entries total 7 hours 35 minutes, not 7 hours 5 minutes.

Accessibility discussion

Looking beyond appearance

SituationI was learning the difference between making a page look good and making it usable for more people.

TaskDiscuss why accessibility matters and identify practical design choices.

ActionI considered contrast, image descriptions, readable content, and keyboard access in my response.

Result & learningI now have specific things to check in future web assignments. I still need practice testing a complete site with a keyboard and accessibility tools.
